Which semiconductor lots should be held for inspection?

590 sensors per lot, a weak signal and a process that changes month to month. The system ranks lots by risk, sets the threshold by cost and reports the saving with its uncertainty.

How the decision was made

  1. Chronological split; expanding-window validation inside training.
  2. Sensor cleaning fitted only on each fold's past.
  3. Threshold by expected cost, not by F1.
  4. One holdout evaluation, stress tests and drift monitoring.

What it does not claim

  • The saving is not statistically demonstrated.
  • Sensor names are anonymous; SHAP rankings are leads, not causes.
  • Costs are assumptions in the configuration.
